## Build a responsible application

### 1 of 11 · 474 / 637

Application connects a passage’s meaning with a concrete response in present life. James supplies a setting for practicing this connection because he joins hearing with doing. His concerns include listening, speech, anger, self-deception, and care for vulnerable people. Identifying genre or vocabulary does not complete the reading when the passage itself asks how reception takes practical form. Read these concerns as parts of the argument rather than disconnected rules.

## Keep the setting visible

### 5 of 11 · 583 / 637

Make an application specific enough to carry out and open enough to revise. In a hostile discussion, someone might listen before answering, check an accusation, and repair a harmful statement. When a neighbor needs support, someone might ask what help is wanted and arrange it. These responses follow the passage’s concerns while respecting the affected person’s account of their own needs. Neither gives permission to control that person.

Canonical Shelf distinguishes receiving salvation by grace from earning it through achievement.
