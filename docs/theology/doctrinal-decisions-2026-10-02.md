# Canonical Shelf --- Doctrinal Decisions Record

**Purpose:** Durable record of doctrinal decisions settled during the
2026-10-02 Statement of Faith reconciliation interview.\
**Rule:** Public Statement of Faith is the doctrinal ceiling. Internal
context may add nuance but may not silently contradict it.\
**Status vocabulary:** AFFIRM / REVISE / OPEN / INTERNAL ONLY.

  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ID                                              Status                  Decision
  ----------------------------------------------- ----------------------- -------------------------------
  theology.god.source-agency                      AFFIRM                  One God is Creator/source of
                                                                          existence; created beings
                                                                          possess genuine agency; God
                                                                          does not determine every choice
                                                                          or directly cause every action.

  theology.god.love                               AFFIRM                  God is Love; Love is
                                                                          foundational to God's nature
                                                                          and creation's purpose; God
                                                                          does not ultimately abandon
                                                                          what God loves.

  theology.god.one-consciousness                  REVISE                  One God, one divine
                                                                          consciousness.
                                                                          Father/Son/Spirit are not three
                                                                          separate divine consciousnesses
                                                                          or literal divine genealogy.
                                                                          This differs from classical
                                                                          three-person formulation.

  theology.god.manifestation                      AFFIRM                  God may physically manifest in
                                                                          multiple forms/places; lack of
                                                                          physical manifestation does not
                                                                          mean absence.

  theology.christ.preexistence                    AFFIRM                  Divine reality embodied as

                                                                          creation; precise pre-incarnate
                                                                          mode is unknown.

                                                                          without encompassing entirety
                                                                          of God; incarnation gave God
                                                                          deeper experiential
                                                                          understanding of human
                                                                          decision. God can
                                                                          learn/respond/change action.

  theology.christ.death-resurrection              AFFIRM                  Jesus was beaten, crucified,
                                                                          truly died, defeated death,
                                                                          bodily rose, appeared,
                                                                          ascended, and will physically
                                                                          return.

  theology.christ.ascension                       OPEN                    Ascension affirmed; mechanics,
                                                                          current physical location, and

                                                                          reconciliation rests on what

                                                                          not a separate consciousness.

  theology.knowledge.open-future                  REVISE                  God knows every possible path

## Required implementation effects

1.  Replace classical-Trinity language that binds Canonical Shelf to
    "three distinct persons" with the settled one-God/one-consciousness
    position while accurately teaching the classical view as a historic
    interpretation.
2.  Remove claims of exhaustive settled divine foreknowledge where they
    conflict with the open-actual-choice model.
3.  Remove "ultimate renewal of creation" as a binding belief; final
    physical state of creation is OPEN.
4.  Preserve universal resurrection while distinguishing it from
    reconciliation.
5.  Preserve eventual universal reconciliation as freely chosen rather
    than coerced.
6.  Update sin language so harm is not itself the definition of sin.
7.  Update repentance and forgiveness language to the settled
    definitions.
8.  Add baptism and explicit open-table belief.
9.  Remove denominational identity references.
10. Keep public doctrine compact; keep speculative mechanisms,
    countertexts, and departures from historic consensus in internal
    context.
11. Do not introduce reincarnation into the reconciled model.
12. Preserve open questions as open rather than supplying a traditional
    default.
