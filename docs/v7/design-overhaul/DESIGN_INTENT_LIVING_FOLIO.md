# Design Intent — Living Folio Site-Wide Baseline

## Human decision
The Living Folio lesson-prototype screenshot is the selected visual direction for Canonical Shelf **as an entire application environment**, not merely as the styling of lesson content.

## Visual grammar to preserve
- dark espresso / near-black full-screen application environment;
- large warm ivory folios and scholarly objects inset within that environment;
- restrained oxblood, bronze, and gilt accents;
- literary serif hierarchy for reading and titles;
- compact uppercase utility labels for orientation and metadata;
- thin rules, subtle borders, restrained rounding, and low-noise depth;
- a strong distinction between global chrome, primary scholarly object, and contextual apparatus;
- high information density at the margins while protecting the reading surface;
- motion used to reveal state, progression, relationship, or transition rather than decoration.

## Product translation
Canonical Shelf should feel like one premium digital reading room in which different scholarly objects open:
- **Home:** a reading-room entry state, not a generic dashboard;
- **Learn:** course volumes, progress paths, and mastery/retention structures inside the same room;
- **Lesson / Study Focus:** Living Folio baseline with scene progression and scholarly margins;
- **Bible:** signature bookshelf that can transition into book profiles and reader folios;
- **Practice:** a focused challenge/retrieval stage with retention state;
- **Topics:** archival/editorial reference objects rather than generic cards;
- **Theologian:** a research desk with conversational synthesis, sources, interpretation, and limits clearly separated.

## What is not selected yet
The visual language is selected. Page composition remains unresolved. Each major surface must receive at least three materially distinct interactive composition choices inside this fixed visual system before production implementation.

## Non-goals
- do not convert the product into corporate SaaS chrome;
- do not use the dark environment as an excuse for a generic AI-chat aesthetic;
- do not flatten every surface into identical cards;
- do not replace the Bible shelf with a purely abstract data visualization;
- do not simplify rich learning interactions for implementation convenience;
- do not write production redesign code until the per-surface composition gate is complete.
