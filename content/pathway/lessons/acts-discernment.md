## Acts 15: a community works through disagreement {#orient}
> The community reasons through a real dispute. Testimony, Scripture, and the question being asked all matter.

The dispute concerns what is required of Gentile believers. Acts does not describe a community free from conflict or difficult questions about inherited practice. The disagreement is serious enough to require a shared process of discernment.

## Read the passage with the question in view {#read}
::reading

## Locate the claim in context {#locate-the-claim-in-context}
The account includes debate, testimony about God’s work, and an appeal to Scripture. These elements should be identified separately before describing how they work together. A conclusion is not merely announced without the narrative giving reasons for it.

The resolution addresses a specific community and question. It should not be treated as a blank authorization for any later proposal, nor as proof that every modern disagreement is identical to this one. An analogy requires both similarities and differences.

## Keep the setting visible {#keep-the-setting-visible}
For contemporary discussion, practice stating the actual question, hearing evidence, representing opposing concerns fairly, and explaining the decision’s scope. Disagreement need not be hidden for a community to learn, but a process should remain attentive to the people whose belonging is being discussed.
